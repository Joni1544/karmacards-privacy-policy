# Privacy Policy

**Karmacards – The Card Game**  
Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Karmacards – The Card Game (the "App") processes information when you use the App.

## 1. Controller and contact

The person responsible for the App is Jonathan Fürst, Friedrichshafen, Germany. For privacy questions and requests, email **jonathanfuerst2003@gmail.com**.

## 2. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, the App and its service providers may process:

- Account and profile information, such as a Unity player ID, username or display name, authentication information, contact information if you provide it, and an optional profile image.
- Game information, such as progress, inventory, virtual coins and diamonds, purchases, friends, multiplayer matches, lobbies and related activity.
- Device and usage information, such as device and app identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app events, diagnostics, consent choices and ad interactions. Advertising identifiers are used only where permitted.
- Purchase information needed to provide in-app products, such as product and transaction status. Apple handles payment-card details; we do not receive your full card number.

Some information is stored on your device. Account, game and multiplayer information may also be transmitted to and stored by the service providers listed below. The App is therefore not an offline-only service.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to provide and secure accounts, save game progress, enable friends and multiplayer, deliver in-app purchases, show and measure ads, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems and improve the App. Where applicable, processing is based on performance of the service requested by you, our legitimate interests in security and service operation, legal obligations, or your consent for advertising and related processing when consent is required.

## 4. Service providers and advertising

The App uses **Unity Technologies** for services that may include Authentication, Cloud Save, Friends, Lobby/Relay/Multiplayer and In-App Purchasing. It uses **Apple** for App Store purchases. For advertising and consent management it uses **Google AdMob** and Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)**. AdMob may use mediation partners configured for the App, including **DT Exchange/Fyber, InMobi and ironSource/Unity Ads**.

These providers may process the information needed for their services under their own privacy notices. For advertising, this may include an IP address, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, approximate location, consent status, usage data and ad interactions. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Where required, the App requests consent before relevant advertising processing. You can change iOS tracking permission in your device Settings. For questions about, or requests to withdraw, advertising consent, contact us at the email above.

Provider privacy information: [Unity](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. In-app purchases

The App offers optional purchases of virtual items or currency. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use the purchase result to provide the purchased content. We do not receive your full payment-card information. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to payment processing.

## 6. Storage, deletion and international transfers

We keep information only for as long as needed to operate the App and account, provide purchases, maintain security, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. The period depends on the data and purpose. Account and Cloud Save data are generally kept while an account remains active. Limited transaction, security or backup records may remain longer where necessary or required by law.

You can request deletion by email. The App also provides an account-deletion option in Settings. This deletes the Unity Authentication account and associated Karma Cards Cloud Save game/profile data, as well as local app data handled by that feature. Other providers may retain limited records under their own legal obligations. Deletion cannot be undone.

Service providers may process data outside your country. Where required, recognized safeguards are used for such transfers.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us.

## 9. Security and changes

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no online service is completely secure. We may update this policy when the App, its providers or legal requirements change. The current version is available at this page and in the App.
